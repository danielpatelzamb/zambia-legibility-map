RESULTS OF THE 75TH MINING LICENSING COMMITTEE (MLC) MEETING HELD FROM 27TH TO 29TH SEPTEMBER 2023

REPUBLIC OF ZAMBIA 

MINISTRY OF MINES AND MINERALS DEVELOPMENT 

MINING CADASTRE DEPARTMENT 

PUBLIC NOTICE 

SUBJECT:   RESULTS OF THE 75TH MINING LICENSING COMMITTEE (MLC)                    MEETING HELD FROM 27TH TO 29TH SEPTEMBER 2023  THE MINING CADASTRE DEPARTMENT WISHES TO INFORM THE GENERAL PUBLIC THAT THE RESULTS OF THE 75TH MINING LICENCING COMMITTEE MEETING ARE NOW OUT AND AVAILABLE AT MINING CADASTRE DEPARTMENT, ALL REGIONAL BUREAUX (MKUSHI, KITWE, CHIPATA, CHOMA, MANSA AND SOLWEZI) AND THE MINISTERIAL WEBSITE & FACEBOOK PAGES. THEREFORE, ALL THOSE WHOSE APPLICATIONS WERE CONSIDERED DURING THE MEETING ARE HEREBY ADVISED AS FOLLOWS;
  1. SUCCESSFUL APPLICANTS MUST COLLECT OFFER LETTERS FROM OUR REGISTRY AND PAY THE APPLICABLE AREA CHARGES AND MEET ANY OTHER CONDITION(S) (AS THE CASE MIGHT BE) WITHIN THE STIPULATED WINDOW PERIOD AS ACKNOWLEDGMENT OF ACCEPTANCE OF THE OFFER;
  1. DEFERRED APPLICANTS MUST PROVIDE THE REQUESTED INFORMATION WITHIN THE STIPULATED WINDOW PERIOD OR MUST EXPLAIN, IN WRITING TO THE DIRECTOR OF MINING CADASTRE, THE STEPS THEY ARE TAKING TO ENSURE THE NECESSARY INFORMATION IS PROVIDED TO ENABLE COMPLETION OF THE LICENCING PROCESS; AND
  1. REJECTED APPLICANTS, WHO INTEND TO APPEAL AGAINST THE DECISION OF THE MINING LICENCING COMMITTEE MUST EXERCISE THEIR RIGHT OF APPEAL IN ACCORDANCE WITH THE PROVISION OF SECTION 97 OF THE MINES AND MINERALS DEVELOPMENT ACT NO. 11 OF 2015 WITHIN THE STIPULATED WINDOW PERIOD.
ALL APPLICANTS ARE ADVISED TO TAKE NOTE OF THE “WINDOW PERIOD” WITHIN WHICH THE OFFER IS VALID, INFORMATION MUST BE PROVIDED (IN CASE OF DEFERRED APPLICATIONS) OR THE APPEAL MUST BE MADE (IN CASE OF REJECTED APPLICATIONS). APPLICANTS SHOULD FURTHER TAKE NOTE THAT, AT THE EXPIRY OF THE RESPECTIVE “WINDOW PERIODS” THE DEPARTMENT WILL PROCEED AS FOLLOWS;
  1. OUTSTANDING OFFERS WILL BE TREATED AS REJECTED/LAPSED AND THE SUBJECT AREAS WILL, ACCORDINGLY, BE CLEARED FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIPT OF FRESH APPLICATIONS;
  1. OUTSTANDING DEFERRED APPLICATIONS, WITHOUT ACCEPTABLE WRITTEN EXPLANATIONS, AS DIRECTED ABOVE, WILL BE PRESENTED IN THE NEXT MINING LICENCING COMMITTEE MEETING FOR POSSIBLE REJECTION; AND
  1. ALL AREAS SUBJECT TO REJECTED APPLICATIONS, WITHOUT PENDING APPEALS TO THE MINISTER, WILL BE TREATED AS AVAILABLE FOR FRESH APPLICATIONS AND WILL, ACCORDINGLY, BE CLEARED AS SUCH.

ISSUED BY SECRETARIAT OF MINING LICENSING COMMITTEE

3RD OCTOBER, 2023, LUSAKA.
