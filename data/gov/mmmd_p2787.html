CANCELLATION OF NON-COMPLIANT MINING AND NON-MINING RIGHTS BY THE MINING LICENSING COMMITTEE (MLC)

REPUBLIC OF ZAMBIA

The Mines and Minerals Development Act, 2015

(Act No. 11 of 2015)

The Mines and Minerals Development (General) Regulations, 2016

PUBLIC NOTICE

CANCELLATION OF NON-COMPLIANT MINING AND NON-MINING RIGHTS BY THE MINING LICENSING COMMITTEE (MLC)

Reference is made to the above subject matter.

In accordance with Section 72 of the Mines and Minerals Development Act No. 1 1 of 201 5, the following mining and non-mining rights were cancelled by the Mining Licensing Committee (MLC) at its 78th meeting for non-compliance to conditions of grant of the mining and non-mining rights which were clearly highlighted in the default notices that were circulated on 24th October, 2023 and did not take remedial measures to the satisfaction of the Committee

In accordance with Section 97 of the Mines and Minerals Development Act No. 1 1 of 2015, if aggrieved by the decision of the Mining Licensing Committee, mining and non-mining right holders may appeal to the Honorable Minister of Mines and Minerals Development within thirty (30) days of receipt of this notice (2nd April, 2024). For efficiency, all appeal letters should be submitted via mining.cadastre@mmmd.gov.zm,  not later than 1st May, 2024. Please note that any appeal that will be submitted later than 1st May, 2024 will be rendered null and void. Cancellation letters can be collected at Mining Cadastre Department and a copy will be sent electronically to the company registered email address starting from 2nd April, 2024. Companies that may have recently changed their email addresses are advised to update the department accordingly.

Take this as official notification.

Samuel C. Maango

Director
