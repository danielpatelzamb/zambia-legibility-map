Licences and revocations

How to read the flags. Amber means one adverse flag, red means two. Every parcel shown is on the active register, so none is currently cancelled.

The June 2025 default notice is a show-cause notice for curable breaches such as unpaid area charges or missing reports, with a 30 day remedy window. Being listed is a flag to check, not proof of default, and a holder still on the register most likely cured it. Absence from this snapshot proves nothing.

    Registry standing

    All 2,716 organisational holders, checked against PACRA's public companies registry: beneficial ownership, annual returns, and minimum share capital.

    One number was corrected downward. An earlier pass counted 559 holders as absent from the companies registry. PACRA's search cannot handle ampersands: VS Construction & Mining Services Limited returns nothing while VS Construction returns it (registered 2008, active). Of the 559, 229 are cooperatives that register with the Registrar of Cooperatives instead, and 116 were registered all along. 214 are genuinely unexplained. 121 further companies are excluded from the declared-business figures because the registry returned several candidate entities and the match was left unresolved rather than guessed.

    Declared business against mineral rights

    Two government registers disagree about who mines. The companies registry says 732 of 2,128 holders are in wholesale trade, construction, freight or farming. The licence register says they hold mineral rights.

    Treat this as a fact about the registry, not the companies. The classification is a form field, not an audit, and it is demonstrably wrong: Rio Tinto Exploration Zambia is filed as Construction of buildings. Excluding holders whose own name contradicts their filing, the defensible figure is 27.7% of licensed ground.

    Largest holdings on a non-mining declaration

    Minerals held under a non-mining declaration

    Line of business comes from the companies registry. Minerals come from the licence register. Where they disagree: 1,015 copper and 911 gold entries sit with companies that told the state they do wholesale trade, construction, freight or farming.

    minerals with ≥20 such entries
    This measures registry quality as much as company behaviour. A construction firm can legitimately hold a limestone right, and a farming company can own ground with copper under it, but the larger caveat is that the classification itself is unreliable. 96 of the 732 (13.1%) carry a mining word in their own registered name while being classified as something else. That is a measurable floor on the error rate, not a ceiling: it cannot catch a mis-filed holder whose name is not self-describing: Willianda Investments, filed under "processing and preserving of fruit and vegetables" against 177,581 ha of iron ore, niobium and cobalt, is exactly that case. Filter on name_contradicts_declaration in declared_business_vs_licences.csv to exclude the detectable ones.

    Who can be found, by licence type

    Opacity is not uniform. Production and processing holders are mostly identifiable. Exploration is thinner. The artisanal tier is dark.

    The Bidding area row gives the game away: 1,442 licences held by 4 holders, because gazetted blocks sit with the state until allocated.

    Gemstones: 28 licences, or 2,056?

    Both. Only 28 licences were issued under the dedicated Gemstone type, a 2008 category that has lapsed. But 2,056 licences list a gem commodity, across 1,459 holders.

    Gemstones are mined under ordinary exploration and small-scale licences, so counting by licence type understates the sector roughly seventyfold.

    Processing licence holders

    195 licences, 189 holders. Smelters, refineries, leach and SX-EW circuits, gemstone cutting. 152 of the 195 appear in the June 2025 default notice and 35 carry a 2024 cancellation flag.

    Copper production, 1964 to 2025

    National copper output from independence to now (USGS / BGS), with the structural events that bent the curve. 748,000 t at the 1969 state takeover, down to 249,000 t by 2000, back to 890,000 t in 2025. Hover any year for the tonnage and what happened. Cobalt is on the same series switch.

    Licensing Committee decisions

    16,120 holder mentions across 60 official documents: Licensing Committee meetings 70 to 91, the October 2023 extraordinary sitting, and the default and cancellation notices. This makes the licensing system itself legible.

    Offtakes, prepayments and corridors

    31 documented supply-chain arrangements with disclosed value where published. This is the layer shipment manifests cannot see: Zambia is landlocked, so no ocean bill of lading is ever issued here and the shipper on a Durban or Dar manifest is a forwarder, not the mine.

    Value addition: announced vs built

    28 policy instruments, zones, international initiatives and state vehicles aimed at moving Zambia beyond cathode: with announced programme value where published. Read against the geolocated facilities that actually exist.

    Institutional contact routes

    3,622 holders. Published contact channels exist for a small fraction, and for the 229 mining cooperatives, none at all. Four sweeps of the full licensing archive found no phone, email or address for any of them.

    The way in is institutional. 82 reachable offices below, from the national federation down to district councils.

    Why the cooperative column is zero. These societies register with the Registrar of Cooperatives: resolved to sit under the Ministry of Small and Medium Enterprise Development, not Commerce, so no companies-registry record exists. Their artisanal rights, roughly 2 to 33 hectares, fall below the environmental-filing threshold, so they never lodge the documents that would carry a proponent contact. 186 of 229 are located to district, which is what makes the provincial route usable.

    Traders, processors and services

    The licence register shows who holds ground. These 354 companies are the sector around it: traders, gemstone dealers, smelters, drilling and blasting contractors, equipment dealers, assay labs, hauliers, clearing agents, reagent suppliers.

    299 are confirmed on an official register. 78 have operations evidenced beyond the registry. Contact details are held privately and are not in this app.

    How these were found, and what that means. Most came from sweeping PACRA's public registry API by keyword: "DRILLING", "GEMSTONE", "HAULAGE" and so on, which surfaces registered Zambian companies that no directory lists. That is why the majority are marked registry record only: the company provably exists and is classified, but no source beyond the register has been checked. Treat those as a verified universe, not a researched profile. The 78 marked operations evidenced have a company site, accreditation, tender award or news source behind them. PACRA's own business classifications are sometimes registration-form artefacts, one assay lab is filed under "hospital activities", and those are flagged per record rather than repeated as fact.

    Mining cooperatives by province

    229 societies holding artisanal and small-scale rights. Central Province alone holds 53, and the Mkushi Regional Mining Bureau and Mkushi District Council share a town, which makes it the single most efficient point of contact for that cohort.

    Mirror-trade gap

    Zambia's reported exports against the sum of partner countries' reported imports, by HS code. Persistent gaps are the standard misinvoicing signal, but CIF/FOB differences, re-export hubs and landlocked transit all add noise.

    Copper production by company

    Company copper production from the Zambia EITI portal, 2018 to 2023, cross-checked against the ZEITI by-company file. 2024 exists only as a national total of 821,874 t.

    Do not sum the bars. Chambishi Copper Smelter and Chambishi Metals report smelter output, which already includes feed from the mines listed.

    Unit-value spread, cathode against concentrate

    Monthly partner-level unit values, USD per tonne, 2023 to 2024. Cathode is grade-controlled, so its values should cluster tightly. Concentrate varies by grade and contract, so its spread is wide. Residual spread in the grade-controlled product is the suggestive signal.

    Correlational, not causal. Unit-value spread reflects grade, firm size, contract type, destination and incoterms, not only paperwork. See Methodology for the causal DAG and the identification strategy this MVP cannot yet run.

    Annual unit-value spread across partners, by HS code

    P10 / median / P90 of partner-level unit values (USD/t) in Zambia-reported exports. Partners with <20 t excluded. The P90/P10 ratio is the "spread": compare the grade-controlled cathode row against concentrate.

    Verified counterparties

    Verified means checkable against a named public record, not endorsed. Four standards: beneficial ownership (PACRA), payment disclosure (EITI), physical flows on US customs manifests, and LME brand registration.

    Most of the sector clears none of them.

    Supply-chain advisor

    Set the four fields. You get the corridor and port, the counterparty categories the deal needs and who is registered in them, the standards a buyer or lender will ask for, and what to watch out for.

    Bills of lading

    US CBP sea-import manifests naming Zambian shippers: cargo, weight, load port and destination. This is what third-party verification looks like when it exists, a foreign customs service with no stake in the story recorded the container.

    The gap this table proves. These manifests exist only because the US publishes import manifests. Zambia's own export side, the ZRA CD-1 form chain: is not public, so the equivalent record for the ~75% of exports that go to China and the region cannot be checked by anyone outside government. Upstream of the port, nothing here verifies which licence the metal came off, that chain-of-custody layer does not exist yet in any public record.

    Find a supplier

    Search 1,730 companies: every customs clearing agent licensed by the ZRA, plus the firms that supply the mines: reagents, explosives, equipment, drilling, freight, assay labs, fuel, fabrication. Type what you need, or pick a category and a town.

    How to get the contact details. Phone numbers and emails are kept out of this site on purpose, it is public, so anything published here is scrapable. They sit in private/dataset/clearing_agents.csv on the project owner's machine, alongside the ZRA register they came from. Clearing agent details are published by the ZRA itself at zra.org.zm/clearing-agents, so you can look any of them up directly.

    Where the clearing agents are

    Customs clearing is the step most export plans underestimate. Agents cluster at the borders they clear through, so where an agent sits tells you which corridor they actually work. 1,278 of 1,376 are located to a town.

    One thing worth knowing before you sign. 105 of these licensed agents share just 39 email addresses between them, one operator holds up to ten separate licensed companies. That is in the ZRA's own register. If you are comparing quotes from several agents, check you are not comparing the same person to himself.

    Licence lookup

    Enter a licence code. This resolves it to its holder, that holder's standing on the companies registry, and any adverse notice against the licence. It is the join that makes a consignment traceable to ground.

    Build a consignment record

    Pick the licence the ore came off, the lab that assayed it, and the agent clearing it. This produces the provenance record Zambia does not currently require, in a form a buyer or lender can check line by line.

    Origin Gate API

    The same verdict as a JSON endpoint, so it can be called instead of clicked. One URL per licence code, no key, no rate limit, CORS open. It is static files, so there is no server to run and nothing to keep up.

    Limits of the certification-spread claim
    
    
    The motivating claim, a trader's report of a ~2× price spread between no-paperwork and
      clean-paper material: is a single-interview datapoint and confounded. Any spread computed from Comtrade
      unit values is correlational. The DAG below shows the back-door paths.
    
    
    
      
    
    
    
    Clean identification would need a within-firm, within-grade before/after design:
      the same seller, the same product, priced before and after obtaining certification. Comtrade aggregates cannot
      support that design, so this MVP labels every spread it shows as suggestive.
    
  
    

  
    
    
    Mirror-trade method (GFI)
    
    
    Compares Zambia's reported exports against partner-reported imports for the same HS code and period, following Global Financial Integrity's method. GFI flags flows where mirror volumes differ by more than 2.5% and the unit-value ratio falls outside 0.8 to 1.8.

    Known noise: CIF against FOB valuation, re-export hubs, and Zambia's landlocked transit through five neighbours.
    
  
    

  
    
    
    Honest limitations
    
    
      
      
    • Formal-economy bias (the central caveat): Comtrade, EITI, PACRA, the cadastre and the
        clearing-agent list all see only the formal economy. The informal/artisanal streams where the arbitrage is
        largest are invisible by construction: this map illuminates the formal shell and infers the void.
      • 
      
    • Cadastre: the Landfolio portal's ArcGIS query endpoint is login-gated; license markers here
        are illustrative, not cadastre records.
      • 
      
    • Preview-tier trade data: the free Comtrade preview caps 500 records/query; any capped query
        is flagged in the data file metadata.
      • 
      
    • Corridors are schematic: waypoint polylines, not surveyed alignments.
      • 
      
    • Legal transition: the Minerals Regulation Commission Act (No. 14 of 2024) may change portal
        URLs/access; re-verify sources before publication.
